## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: Sleek futuristic Stark HUD meets sovereign local AI engineering
- Interpretation: Ultra-crisp typography, high-contrast cyan glow against deep space black (#00060a), measured rhythmic pacing, and high-tech telemetry elements that feel purposeful and sharp.
- Angle: Your OS was built for typing and clicking. Mark LVI turns your machine into a real-time reactive presence with eyes, ears, and hands — running directly on your computer with zero subscriptions, local memory, and absolute control.
- Hook: An arc-reactor cyan HUD boots in total darkness. Circular telemetry rings spin up and the holographic wireframe avatar illuminates: "INITIALIZING MARK LVI".
- Outro / punchline: "ZERO SUBSCRIPTIONS · TOTAL AUTONOMY. You built the machine — now let it speak."
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language ("Streamline your workflow")
  - Abstract blurry blobs or generic gradient meshes
  - Low contrast text or cluttered elements
  - Exposing any real keys, hostnames, or private secrets
